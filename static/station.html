WSPR reception

WSPR ("whisper") is a radio mode for testing propagation: stations around the world send a tiny beacon, often well under 1 watt, every few minutes, and receivers like this one decode it even when it is far below the noise. Here is what this station heard in the last 24 hours.

Aircraft tracking (ADS-B)

Airliners broadcast their position, altitude and speed on 1090 MHz several times a second. This station receives those messages and shares them with flight-tracking networks, helping them cover the region.
